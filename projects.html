Large-scale NLP pipeline · Python, Hadoop, BERT, Scikit-learn

  • Handled end-to-end data wrangling with Pandas and NumPy — tokenization, null handling, and feature transformation to prepare model-ready inputs.
  • Fine-tuned BERT embeddings for sentiment classification (positive/negative/neutral), improving precision over a TF-IDF baseline.
